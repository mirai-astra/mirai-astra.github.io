01

Who this policy covers

This Policy explains how the Mirai Astra Studio independent desktop project (“Studio”, “we” or “us”) handles information when you use the desktop app, authorize a connected platform or visit this information website. For privacy matters, the project operator can be contacted at apferdick1@gmail.com.

The desktop app runs locally on the user’s computer. This public website supplies product and policy information; it is not the desktop app and does not receive platform tokens or video uploads.

03

Purposes and legal bases

The Studio uses information to provide the account connection, local library, upload tracking and statistics features you request, protect those operations and respond to you. Information obtained from TikTok is used for those requested integration features.

Where the GDPR or similar law applies, the relevant bases are performance of the requested service, consent for optional platform permissions where required, legitimate interests in security and responding to support, and compliance with legal obligations where applicable. You may withdraw consent by revoking access; this does not affect prior lawful processing.

We do not sell or rent platform data, use it for targeted advertising, or use connected-account data to train AI models. The described account integration does not make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you.

04

Local storage and security

The desktop app stores account connections, drafts, cached statistics and operation history in a local database on your computer. Platform credentials are encrypted at rest; on Windows the encryption key is protected for the current Windows user. Video files remain in the local folders you use until you choose to transfer them.

The project operator does not automatically receive your desktop database, account tokens or video files. Do not send credentials when requesting support. Device security, operating-system access and backups remain important; encryption does not protect a device that has already been compromised.

05

Transfers and third parties

When you authorize an account, the Studio communicates with the platform’s authorization and API services. When you confirm a video transfer, it sends the selected file and applicable metadata to that platform. TikTok Inbox Upload requires a further action in TikTok to finish the post.

Platforms process those transfers under their own terms and privacy policies. Their infrastructure, the website host and your email provider may process information in countries outside your country of residence, including outside the EEA. Applicable provider safeguards and privacy notices govern their processing.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Hosting and network providers may process technical request information such as IP address, browser details, requested URL and time for delivery, security and operational logging. We do not add advertising trackers, third-party analytics, embedded social feeds or a visitor sign-up form to this site. Provider-level security mechanisms may operate independently.

If you email the project, your email service and the project’s Gmail mailbox process the message. Information may also be disclosed when legally required or necessary to handle a specific security or legal issue.

Provider notices: TikTok Privacy Policy, GitHub Privacy Statement and Google Privacy Policy.

06

Retention

Local account credentials are kept while the connection exists. Disconnecting an account removes that local connection, including its stored tokens, and its cached analytics. Local drafts, publication records, operation history and original video files can remain after disconnection; they are not all erased by that action.

Other local records remain until you remove them or reset the local installation. Local backups may contain older copies until you remove those backups. The project does not maintain a central copy of your local installation.

We retain support messages only as long as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable legal obligations. Requests can ask us to remove correspondence that is no longer needed. Platform and hosting records are retained according to the relevant provider’s own policies and controls.

07

Disconnecting and deleting data

  1. In the Studio, open Accounts, choose the connected account and select Disconnect. Wait for any active upload to finish first.
  2. In TikTok, open the security settings for Apps and services permissions (wording may vary), select Mirai Astra Studio and remove its access. This revokes authorization independently of the local Studio connection.
  3. Remove unwanted local drafts and files using the Studio’s available controls and your computer’s file manager. For a complete local reset, close the Studio and remove its local application state and any backups you no longer need. Ask us for the appropriate steps for your installation.
  4. Delete or manage videos already uploaded to TikTok from TikTok itself. Disconnecting the Studio does not delete TikTok posts or inbox uploads.

To request deletion of support correspondence or other information held directly by the project operator, email apferdick1@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request”. Identify the relevant account or correspondence without sending your password or access token.

08

Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or obtain a portable copy of your personal data, object to certain processing and withdraw consent. Contact us to exercise rights over information we hold. We may need proportionate information to verify that a request relates to you.

We respond within the time required by applicable law, generally within one month for GDPR requests, subject to permitted extensions. You may complain to your competent data protection authority. For information held directly by a connected platform or hosting provider, use that provider’s privacy request process.

09

Children and changes to this policy

The Studio is not directed at children under 13 or below the applicable minimum age for a connected platform. Do not use an integration if you do not meet its age requirements. Contact us if you believe a child has supplied personal information directly to the project.

We may update this Policy when the app, integrations or handling of information changes. We will publish the revised text and updated date here. A materially new purpose for platform data will require the appropriate notice and authorization before use.
