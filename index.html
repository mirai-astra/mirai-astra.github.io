A CLEAR PATH TO POSTING

Your content.
Your account. Your call.

Prepare locally, connect through the platform, and review what you send.

  1. 01

    Prepare your video

    Organize local video files, preview a version and edit the accompanying caption before uploading.

  2. 02

    Connect your account

    Authorize the desktop app through TikTok’s official sign-in flow. The studio does not ask for your TikTok password.

  3. 03

    Review and share

    Confirm the upload in the studio. With TikTok Inbox Upload, open the notification in TikTok to finish editing and publish.
