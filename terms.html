Uploads and publication

Review the account, video version, caption and available settings before confirming an upload. The Studio starts an upload only after the relevant user confirmation or a previously confirmed scheduled action.

The TikTok Inbox Upload workflow sends your selected video to TikTok for review. You must open the TikTok notification, complete any edits and finish publication inside TikTok. Uploading a file through this workflow does not itself create a public post, and the caption may need to be pasted in TikTok.

Direct Post is a separate platform capability requiring appropriate permissions and compliance with TikTok’s review and audit rules. Its presence in the desktop interface does not mean it is approved or available. Sandbox and unaudited access may be restricted.

Platform processing, moderation, rate limits and availability may delay or prevent an upload or publication. The Studio does not guarantee publication, reach, engagement, earnings or platform approval.
